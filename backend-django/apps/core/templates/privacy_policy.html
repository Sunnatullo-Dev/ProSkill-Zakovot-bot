Key Privacy Highlights

Zakovat App is an intellectual trivia and educational competition platform. We only collect the minimal personal data necessary to authenticate your account, maintain fair leaderboard competition, and preserve your game statistics. We never sell your personal data or share it with data brokers.

1. Introduction and Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how Zakovat App ("we", "our", or "the Platform") collects, uses, stores, and protects personal information when you use our intellectual competition platform.

Zakovat App operates across multiple unified environments:

  • Mobile Applications: The native Flutter applications for Android (Google Play Store) and iOS.
  • Backend Services: The Django REST API backend located at zakovat-backend-euc8.onrender.com.
  • Telegram Interfaces: The official Telegram bot (@ZakovatOyinBot) and the associated React-based Telegram Mini App.

By creating an account, accessing, or participating in quizzes through any of these clients, you acknowledge the collection and handling of your information as set forth in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

We strictly adhere to data minimization principles. We collect only information that is directly necessary to deliver quiz games, calculate standings, and maintain account security.

A. Account and Profile Information

  • Phone Number: Collected exclusively when registering via the mobile application. Used as your primary account identifier and login credential. We do not use your phone number for marketing calls or send unsolicited SMS.
  • Password (Hashed): When you create an account on mobile, your password is encrypted using strong one-way cryptographic algorithms (PBKDF2/Argon2 with individual salts). We never store or transmit your password in plain text.
  • Profile Names: Your first name, optional last name, and chosen display name or pseudonym shown on public leaderboards.
  • Telegram Identifier: When using the Telegram bot or Telegram Mini App, or when voluntarily linking your Telegram account from the mobile app, we record your public Telegram user ID (telegram_id) and public username.

B. Gameplay, Competition, and Progress Records

  • Quiz Responses: Your selected answers, timestamps of submission, and whether the answer was correct.
  • Scores and Leaderboards: Accumulated competition points, monthly league rankings, daily challenge streak counters, and unlocked achievements.
  • Multiplayer Activity: Game room participation codes, Svoyak buzzer reaction timings, 1v1 battle challenge history, Zakovat Table team roles (Captain, Player, Spectator), and in-team collaboration messages.
  • User-Submitted Questions: If you voluntarily author and submit trivia questions for review, we store the question text, suggested answers, and your contributor attribution.
  • Voluntary Premium Receipts: If you request a voluntary premium status, you may upload a payment receipt screenshot for manual administrative review.

C. Device, Technical, and Diagnostic Data

  • IP Address: Logged temporarily in server access logs and security caches strictly for rate limiting, brute-force defense, and DDoS mitigation.
  • Timestamps & Server Clock Calibration: Request times are processed via /api/time to synchronize game timers and ensure anti-cheat round timing fairness across international devices.
  • Client Identifiers: Standard HTTP request headers (such as User-Agent) used for server diagnostic logging and platform compatibility verification.

D. Telegram Web App Data

When using the Telegram Mini App, Telegram provides cryptographically signed init data (initData). We verify this signature on our backend using HMAC-SHA256. We do not access, view, or read your private Telegram chats, contacts, or messages outside of our bot.

4. Account Creation, Authentication, and Account Linking

Zakovat App enables players to maintain one unified identity across the Flutter mobile apps, Telegram bot, and Telegram Mini App:

  • Mobile Authentication: Handled through secure JSON Web Tokens (JWT). Short-lived access tokens (30 minutes) are stored in secure on-device hardware storage, and refresh tokens (60 days) are rotated upon use.
  • Cryptographic Account Linking Protocol: When you connect your mobile account to Telegram, we generate a one-time 256-bit cryptographically random token (secrets.token_urlsafe(32)). This token expires after 15 minutes and is stored in our database only as a SHA-256 hash.
  • Zero-Knowledge Linking: You confirm this token inside the Telegram bot (/start link_<token>). At no point are your mobile phone number, password, or authentication credentials transmitted in open Telegram chat messages.
  • Data Merge: Upon confirmation, your previous scores, tournament records, and achievements are unified into your single central account.

5. Telegram Integration

Our bot and Mini App operate within the Telegram platform provided by Telegram Messenger Inc.

  • We only access information that you transmit to the bot or that Telegram provides upon launch (your Telegram ID, first name, last name, and username).
  • We do not request or access your private Telegram messages, your contacts list, or your personal device storage through Telegram.
  • Telegram's own data processing practices are governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.

6. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

Our Commitment on Data Monetization

We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, or monetize your personal information to third parties, advertising networks, or data brokers under any circumstances.

We share minimal necessary data only with trusted infrastructure providers required to operate our platform:

  • Render (Cloud Hosting & Database): Our Django backend API and database are hosted on Render Services, Inc. Render provides secure data center infrastructure and automated backups.
  • Telegram (Bot & Mini App Platform): Telegram Messenger Inc. facilitates bot messaging and Mini App web views.
  • Google Gemini AI (Server-Side Evaluation): We use Google Gemini API strictly on our backend for automated question validation and audio response evaluation. Only the question text and answer text are sent to the AI service; no personal user identifiers (such as names or phone numbers) are included in AI requests.
  • Legal Disclosures: We may disclose information only if required by law, subpoena, or valid judicial process to protect legal rights or ensure user safety.

7. Hosting and Data Storage

All backend databases and application logic are hosted in enterprise cloud environments provided by Render. Data is stored in managed relational databases with access restricted to authorized operational staff through cryptographic SSH keys and environment secrets.

All communication between our clients (mobile apps, web apps, bot) and the backend API is strictly encrypted in transit using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).

8. Data Retention Policy

  • Active Accounts: We retain account credentials, profile details, and gameplay records as long as your account remains active so that you can view your competition history and maintain your rank.
  • Transient Security Tokens: One-time account linking tokens expire and are invalidated after 15 minutes. Ephemeral answer validation tickets expire within 120 seconds.
  • Session Invalidation: When you tap "Log Out" or "Log Out from All Devices", access and refresh tokens are immediately revoked and blacklisted.
  • Inactive Accounts: Accounts with no activity for more than 24 consecutive months may be archived or scheduled for deletion following notice.

9. Account Deletion Instructions

In full accordance with Google Play Store User Data policies, users have the right to request the complete deletion of their account and all associated personal data at any time.

How to Request Deletion:

  1. In-App Deletion: In the mobile application, navigate to Sozlamalar (Settings) → Akkaunt → Akkauntdan chiqish / Akkauntni o'chirish, or submit a request directly through the app support interface.
  2. Email Deletion Request: You may submit an account deletion request by emailing us at support@zakovat-app.uz with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". Please include your registered phone number or Telegram User ID to verify ownership.

What Happens Upon Deletion:

  • Your personal credentials, phone number, hashed password, and personal profile information are permanently erased from our active database within 30 days.
  • Past quiz questions you authored and collective leaderboard score totals are permanently decoupled from your identity and retained in anonymized format to preserve historical contest rankings.

10. Data Security Safeguards

We maintain comprehensive technical and organizational safeguards:

  • Password Hashing: Passwords are cryptographically hashed using PBKDF2 with SHA-256 and salt. Passwords are never stored or logged in plain text.
  • Hardware Storage on Mobile: Mobile JWT tokens are securely stored using FlutterSecureStorage, utilizing Android KeyStore (EncryptedSharedPreferences) on Android and Keychain Services on iOS.
  • Anti-Tampering Protection: Sensitive in-app screens employ FLAG_SECURE to prevent screen scraping, unauthorized screenshots, and background recording.
  • Rate Limiting & Anti-Brute-Force: Authentication and password change endpoints are guarded by strict rate-limiting policies to prevent credential-stuffing and automated attacks.
  • Constant-Time Password Verification: We implement timing-attack mitigations (dummy hashing) during phone authentication to prevent user enumeration attacks.
